The loop

Every frame: apply gravity to velocity, velocity to position, then check collisions. That's the whole engine — three lines and a handful of ifs.

Which way a brick bounces

On contact the ball overlaps the brick on both axes. Reflecting the axis with the smaller overlap is what makes edge hits and corner hits behave the way your eye expects.

Substepping

A fast ball can cross the paddle between two frames and miss it entirely. The movement is split into small slices so that can't happen.

Splitting

A large rock breaks into two mediums, a medium into two smalls. Small rocks pay 25, large ones only 5 — the points are in the follow-up, not the first shot.

Letting them through

Nothing happens when a rock reaches the floor. Ignoring a large one is often safer than turning it into four fast-moving fragments.

Banking lives

Get the sky back round to black — one full colour cycle — and you earn a life. The backdrop is the clock: watch for orange and you know one is close.

The gunship

From the first grey wave it crosses near the top, either way, firing rounds that pass straight through rock. Worth 150 — more than anything else on screen.

Spinners

The red diamonds end the run if they touch the floor — lives don't save you. But one drifting off the side is harmless, so read the angle before you spend shots on it.
